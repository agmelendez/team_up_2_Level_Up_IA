## MS-01 · Correo de seguimiento a un cliente

**Nivel simulado:** A2  
**Extensión meta:** 80–100 palabras  
**Contexto:** atención al cliente

```text
Subject: Your service request

Hello Mr. Brown,

I write you for give information about your request. Yesterday our technician visit your
office, but he don't find the responsible person. He left a note in the reception. We need
that you confirm a new hour for the visit. The technician can go in Monday at 9:00 or Tuesday
in the afternoon. Please answer this mail with your best option. We are very compromised with
your problem and want resolve it soon.

Regards,
Customer Service
```

## MS-03 · Procedimiento para registrar una reservación

**Nivel simulado:** A2 alto–B1 bajo  
**Extensión meta:** 100–120 palabras  
**Contexto:** turismo y hospitalidad

```text
First, the agent ask the customer for the dates and how many persons will stay. Then, you must
revise if there is rooms available in the system. If the customer want breakfast, add the plan
before calculate the final price. Explain what services are included and say the cancellation
politic. After, request the full name and contact information. The customer must give his credit
card for guarantee the reservation. Finally, repeat all details and send a confirmation by
email. It is important that the agent verifies the information because a mistake can make the
guest lose his room when arrives.
```

```text
We apologize for the delay and the damages that this can cause to your operation. For avoid
future problems, our logistics team is checking the maintenance calendar of all vehicles.
We appreciate your patience and remain attentive to any consultation.

Kind regards,
Logistics Coordinator
```

## MS-06 · Procedimiento ante una llamada difícil

**Nivel simulado:** B1  
**Extensión meta:** 110–140 palabras  
**Contexto:** centro de contacto

```text
When a customer calls angry, the first step is listen without interrupting. The agent should
take notes and identify what is the principal problem. After the customer finish, the agent
must recognize the situation and explain what actions can do. It is important not promise a
solution that depends of another department. If the case needs escalation, the agent should
tell who will contact the customer and in how much time. Before finish the call, summarize the
agreement and ask if the customer has another doubt. Finally, register the case with clear
details. This information permits that the next agent understands the situation and does not
make the customer repeat everything again.
```

diferenciada y una microrremediación. Después compare su análisis con la clave docente.
